### How the mapping was established

Not by filename guesswork — by matching the panel titles in the notebook plotting
code against the figure legends in the manuscript:

- `inferable_gap_species_contribution` sets titles `"C. Could A. thaliana evidence fill
  this inferable gap cell?"` and `"D. Distribution of evidence depth across inferable gap
  cells"` in a 1×3 subplot grid → manuscript Figure 4, panels C–E.
- `metabolic_space_estimate` is a 1×2 grid with the global breakdown and the top-15
  pathway ranking → manuscript Figure 4, panels A–B.
- `bridgedb_identifier_complementation` sets `"B. Cross-reference coverage per database:
  PlantCyc source vs BridgeDb"` → manuscript Figure 5.
- `wikidata_coverage_overlap` is a 2×2 grid setting `"B. Coverage gap concentrates in
  low-frequency (specialist) metabolites"` and `"D. Lineage-specific novel curation
  targets"` → manuscript Figure 6.

**Figure 4 is a composite** of two separately generated files stacked in one float
(A–B above C–E), which reproduces the preprint's two-page layout.

### Deliberately excluded

Two files in the same directory are **supplementary**, not main figures, and the

Figure 1 is constrained by `height=0.64\textheight` rather than `width=\textwidth`:
at full text width its 2.23 aspect ratio produced a 16 × 35.7 cm image against a
24.7 cm text height.

**Its legend was trimmed from 316 to 226 words** to make the image readable — at the
original length the figure could be no more than 5.6 cm wide. The removed material
was script filenames and URLs repeated verbatim from Materials and Methods, now
replaced by a pointer there. The full original legend is in commit `9768a0b`.
